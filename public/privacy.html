eNoVo Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

eNoVo ("eNoVo," "we," "us") is a city-scoped community app for diaspora communities. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using eNoVo you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and/or phone number used to sign in, and a password (stored by our authentication provider, not in plain text).
  • Profile information: display name, avatar, the cultural/ethnic communities you choose to identify with, and your city or area.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, forum messages, direct messages, marketplace and housing listings, business listings, and events.
  • Photos and media you upload (e.g., profile pictures, listing images).
  • Location: approximate or precise location used to show communities, businesses, housing, and events near you. You can control this through your device permissions.
  • Calls: eNoVo supports voice and video calling. Calls are transmitted peer-to-peer (WebRTC) and are not recorded or stored by us.
  • Device and usage data: a push notification token (to deliver notifications), and basic diagnostic/usage information to operate and improve the service.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and authenticate you.
  • To provide core features: discovery, feed, messaging, calling, listings, events, and forums.
  • To show relevant nearby communities, businesses, housing, and events using your location.
  • To send notifications you have enabled (messages, orders/quotes, and other updates).
  • To maintain safety, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that operate the app on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) — hosting, database, file storage, and push notifications.
  • Google Maps Platform — to display maps and location-based results.
  • TURN/relay providers — to relay call media when a direct peer-to-peer connection cannot be established. Call content is not stored by these providers.
  • Other users — content you choose to post publicly or send is visible to its intended audience.

Security

Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at the address below; we will delete or anonymize your data except where we must retain it to comply with the law or resolve disputes.

Children

eNoVo is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Permissions we request

  • Camera and microphone — for voice/video calls and uploading photos.
  • Location — to show nearby communities, businesses, housing, and events.
  • Notifications — to deliver messages and updates you opt into.

Your choices

You can manage location, camera, microphone, and notification permissions in your device settings, edit or delete your content in the app, and request account deletion as described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
